# Responsible research and security

Ghostwriter is dual-use safety research. Its prompts and outputs may contain fabricated evidence, stereotypes, medical misinformation, psychological manipulation, or other harmful material.

## Allowed research context

- controlled red-team evaluations;
- development and assessment of safeguards;
- reproducibility studies on isolated research accounts;
- analysis that does not expose unsuspecting users to generated content.

## Prohibited use

- deployment in public or user-facing chatbots;
- influence operations, harassment, discrimination, fraud, or targeted persuasion;
- generation of medical, legal, financial, or self-harm guidance for real users;
- publishing generated claims as factual evidence;
- attempts to bypass provider terms, access controls, or applicable law.

Use dedicated low-privilege API credentials, set spending limits, avoid logging sensitive prompts, and manually review generated content. Report security concerns privately to the contact listed on the final project page.
